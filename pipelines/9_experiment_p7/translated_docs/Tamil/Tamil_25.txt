Introduction:

A road accident means at least one person being injured or dying in a vehicle accident on the open road. India has one of the worst road accident records in the world, with at least 130,000 deaths every year. Murder, suicide, and natural disasters are not included in road accidents.

Road accidents are a primary cause of death due to injury. They are the tenth leading cause of deaths worldwide. Approximately 1.2 million people die in road accidents each year, and nearly 50 million people are injured.

Every three minutes a child dies in an accident in the world.

Main reasons for deaths in road accidents:

1.  Drunk driving (40%), speeding (24%), increased vehicle numbers (16%), negligence in implementing the law (15%), and low usage of helmets and seat belts (5%).
2.  Distraction to the driver.
3.  Crossing the road when the red light is on.
4.  Overtaking other vehicles in the wrong way.

Various factors for road accidents:

Driver: Speeding, reckless driving, violating road rules, failing to understand traffic signals, fatigue, and drunk driving.

Pedestrians: Carelessness, lack of education, crossing the road in wrong places.

Passengers: Stretching hands and heads outside, talking to the driver, getting on and off the vehicle in the wrong way, traveling on the footboard, boarding a moving vehicle.

Vehicles: Vehicle losing control, tire burst, low visibility, overloading.

Road condition: Potholes, damaged roads, eroded road at the junction of rural road and highway, unauthorized speed breakers.

Weather: Fog, snow, heavy rain, storm, hailstorm.

Rules to ensure road safety for children:

It is essential for children to know road safety rules but do not overload them with information beyond their capacity. Here are some basic road safety rules and activities for children.

Let's see some of the most important ways to teach children:

1.  Know the road safety signs.
2.  Stop! Look! Go!
3.  Look – Think!
4.  Don't run on the road!
5.  Always use the sidewalk!
6.  Don't stick your hand out of the vehicle!
7.  Don't cross the road quickly!
8.  Don't be in a hurry! What is the sound you hear on the road?
9.  Go to the left.
10. Cross the road at pedestrian crossings.

1.  One of the best ways for older children to learn is through crossword puzzles. Road safety education crossword puzzles for children can be found online.

2.  Play the "what does that sound mean" game - ask what road sounds they can hear.

3.  Red light, green light: Have the children run and then when someone says red light, they need to stop running. They can only run when green light is called.
