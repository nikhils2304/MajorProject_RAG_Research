Rural settlements are settlements that primarily

Types of Rural Settlements

Rectangular Patterns

These types of settlements are found in the plain

areas and valley areas. Here the roads are

rectangular in shape and intersect each other at

right angles.

Linear Patterns

These types of settlements are found on the

banks of roads, railway lines, river banks and

levees.

Circular or Semicircular Patterns

These types of settlements are located around

lakes, ponds and coastal areas in circular or semi-

circular shapes.

Star-like Patterns

Star-shaped settlements are found on the edges

of paved or unpaved road intersections. These are

spread in all directions in a star shape.

Triangular Patterns

Settlements located where rivers meet are

triangular settlements.

How to help develop awareness about

sustainable development among students.

• Lifestyle

You can change the lifestyle you choose. For

example, when going to a grocery store, be sure

• Repair

If your toy or camera breaks or malfunctions,

all the time. When using an item, consider

whether it can be recycled/reused.

need it'? Or 'is it your preference'? Sustainable
