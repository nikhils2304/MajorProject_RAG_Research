The Circus of Education

Ayyappa Paniker

If you pass first grade?
I can go to second grade.
If you pass second grade?
If I pass second grade,

Well, I can go straight to third grade.

Well, if I pass third grade too, well then,

Then I can sit in fourth grade.

If you pass all these grades?
If all the grades are finished,
I will still go to the next grade.

All education is a circus

With the help of which we pass to the next grade.
Where did knowledge go?

Where it went is a deception!

Translated by: Utpal Kumar Basu

Ayyappa Paniker (1930-2006): Born in Kovalam, Kerala. Ayyappa Paniker's contribution to creating the modern era in Malayalam poetry is memorable. He was also an essayist and literary critic. He taught in various colleges and universities. The book of poems "Kurukshetram," published in 1960, changed the course of Malayalam poetry. "Ayyappa Panikerude Kritikal" and "Thuva" are among his other notable books.
