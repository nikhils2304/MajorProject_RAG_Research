The smallest portion of a word that is uttered in a single effort of the vocal organs is called a syllable (দল) or অক্ষর (351-18916). Here, the smallest effort means a stress or beat (ঝোঁক). That is, the number of times a stress is needed while pronouncing a word, that is the number of syllables the word contains. Again, some words are pronounced at once or have only one stress; that is, they have only one syllable. Let's clarify the matter with a few examples: The word “ব্যাকরণ” (grammar) requires three stresses to pronounce, ব্যা-ক-রণ্‌. Similarly__

মাছ__ মাছ (one syllable)

দল-__ দল্‌ (one syllable)

ছন্দ_ ছন্দ (two syllables)

বাংলা_ বাং-লা (two syllables)

সম্মোহন_সম্‌-মো-হন্‌ (three syllables)

কলকাতা-কল্‌-কা-তা (three syllables)

ভারতবর্ষ__ ভা-রত্-বর্ষ (four syllables)

রবীন্দ্রনাথ__ র-বীন্দ্র-নাথ্‌ (four syllables)

বিবেকানন্দ__বি-বে-কা-নন্-দ (five syllables)

From the examples above, it is clear that words are formed with one or more syllables. And just as a word can be monosyllabic, it can also be polysyllabic [for example: পাখি (bird) has two syllables, ধান (rice) has one syllable]. These syllables can be divided into two parts: 1. Open syllable (স্বরান্ত বা মুন্তদল) (9790 95118919) 2. Closed syllable (ব্যগ্জনান্ত বা রুদ্ধদল) (01939 951181019).

1. Open syllable (স্বরান্ত বা মুস্তুদল): A syllable that ends with a vowel sound or a syllable that has only one vowel sound, is called an open syllable. For example: আসে -__ আ-সে-_ here both syllables are open. Because they end in a vowel sound, their pronunciation is prolonged, hence they are open syllables.

2. Closed syllable (ব্যগ্জনান্ত বা রুদ্ধদল): A syllable that ends with a consonant sound is called a closed syllable. For example: দুষ্ট __ দুষ্ট-_ here “দুষ” is a closed syllable and “ট” is an open syllable. Similarly, "মিশ", "মাছ", "বিল", "চল", "রাগ" etc. are all closed syllables. No more sounds can be added after a closed syllable, otherwise it becomes two syllables. For example: মন্‌+টা - মনটা (two syllables, মন - closed syllable / টা - open syllable)

Among the vowel sounds, অ, আ etc. are full vowels (পূর্ণস্বর) in open syllables and আর, আউ, আও etc. are compound vowels (যুক্তস্বর) in closed syllables. In other words, a full vowel that can accommodate a half vowel (খণ্ডস্বর) at the end is an open vowel (মুক্তস্বর). But a full vowel that cannot accommodate any more half vowels at the end is a closed syllable. For example, in the syllable “দা”, “ও” can be joined as a half vowel. So “দা” is an open syllable. But in the syllable “দাও”, no more sounds can be added as a half vowel. So “দাও” is a closed syllable.

Now let’s analyze the number of syllables and their type in different words:

আমার (mine) : আ - মার্‌ (two syllables: আ - open syllable, মার্‌ - closed syllable)

আশৈশব (since childhood) : আ-শৈ-শব (three syllables: আ- open syllable, শৈ - open syllable, শব - closed syllable)

গ্রামবাসী (villager) : গ্রাম্‌-বা -সী (three syllables: গ্রাম - closed syllable, বা - open syllable, সী - open syllable)

চন্দ্রবদনা (moon-faced) : চন্‌- দ্রো- ব-দ-না (five syllables: চন্‌ - closed syllable, দ্রো - open syllable, ব - open syllable, দ - open syllable, না - open syllable)

নির্মল (clean) : নির্‌ - মল (two syllables: নির্‌ - closed syllable, মল - closed syllable)
