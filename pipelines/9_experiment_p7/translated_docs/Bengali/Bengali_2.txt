Satyanath Bhaduri (1906-1965): Born in Purnia, Bihar. After obtaining a postgraduate degree in economics, he passed law and practiced law with his lawyer father for some time. Later, leaving that work, he joined the Congress and started doing constructive work in the villages of Purnia. Joining the national movement, he spent a long time in prison between 1940 and 1944. He left the Congress in 1948 and joined the Socialist Party. The novel "Jagari", written in 1945, brought him fame. He also received the Rabindra Prize for this book (1950). His other notable books include "Torai Charitmanas" "Chitragupta's File", etc. His short stories are equally popular for their strong narrative and language. Among his story collections are Ganayak, Aparichita, Chakachaki, Patralekha's Father, Jalabhumi, etc. The reflection of social commitment can be seen in his stories.
